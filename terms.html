Who can play online

Online play — public games, chat and voice — is for players aged 13 and over. Under that age, please stick to the single-player modes.

How to behave

The rule is simple: do not use chat, voice or your nickname to harass, threaten, demean or sexualise anybody.

Specifically, do not: harass, threaten or abuse other players; post hateful content targeting anyone on the basis of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age; share sexual content, or any content involving minors; impersonate another player or claim to run the game; share other people's personal information; advertise or spam; or use a nickname that does any of the above. Nicknames are filtered for characters, not for meaning — an offensive one will be removed when reported.

Fair play

Do not modify the game client to gain an advantage, submit leaderboard scores for runs you did not play, or attempt to disrupt the server or other players' connections. Scores that fail verification are rejected, and entries found to be fabricated are removed.

Voice and chat are between players

Voice audio travels directly between players and is never recorded or stored by us. Chat messages are relayed and not kept after a match. That means we cannot review the contents of a past conversation. Use the tools in the game: mute an individual player, deafen yourself, leave voice entirely, or — if you are the host — turn voice off for the whole game. If someone is behaving badly, leave the game and report it to the address below with the nickname and roughly when it happened.

Enforcement

We can remove leaderboard entries, block connections and restrict access to online features where these terms are broken. Because there are no accounts, enforcement is necessarily limited — which is why the in-game mute, deafen and host controls are the tools you should reach for first.
