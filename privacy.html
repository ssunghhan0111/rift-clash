Privacy Policy

RIFT CLASH · last updated 26 July 2026

RIFT CLASH is a browser game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking. This page describes everything the game actually stores or transmits, in plain terms.

The short version. We keep a nickname you choose, a temporary record of your network address for abuse control, and — if you post one — your Survival score. Voice chat is peer-to-peer, which means the people you play with can see your IP address. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

What is stored on your own device

The game uses your browser's local storage for settings only. That data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser: the nickname you chose, your personal best score for each Survival difficulty and for each day's challenge, whether you opted out of fullscreen, whether you turned voice chat off, and — during an online match — a temporary token that lets you rejoin if your connection drops. The rejoin token is held in session storage and disappears when you close the tab.

What the server receives

When you play online, the server receives the nickname you chose, the faction, map and mode you pick, and the in-game commands you issue during a match. It also sees your network (IP) address, as any web server does. IP addresses are held only in memory and only to enforce connection and rate limits; they are not written to the score file and are discarded when the connection closes or the limit window expires.

If you type in the chat box, that message is relayed to the other players in your game. Messages are not stored after the match ends and are not logged.

The leaderboard

Posting a Survival score is optional and always a deliberate action — nothing is sent unless you press Submit. A posted score stores the nickname you entered, the wave you reached, the number of enemies defeated, your faction, whether the run was solo or co-op, and the time of submission. It is displayed publicly on the in-game leaderboard. Choose a nickname you are happy for strangers to see; there is no requirement to use a real name.

To keep the board honest, a run is opened with the server when it begins and the finished run's wave timings are checked against it. Those timings are used for that check and are not retained afterwards.

Voice chat — please read this part

Voice chat uses WebRTC, which connects players directly to each other. The audio never passes through our server. The unavoidable consequence is that anyone you are on a voice call with can, in principle, see your IP address — this is true of every peer-to-peer voice system, including the ones built into other games and chat apps.

Because of that, and because a public game can be joined by anyone:

In public games, your microphone stays off until you deliberately press Join Voice. In private and invited games — where you already chose who you are playing with — voice starts automatically, and you can turn that off permanently by pressing Leave Voice once. You can mute any individual player without deafening yourself to everyone else, and the host can disable voice for a whole game. No audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted to us.

Your browser will ask for microphone permission before voice can start. If you refuse it, everything else in the game works normally.

Children

Online play — including chat and voice — is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Single-player modes need no network features at all. If you believe a child under 13 has posted a nickname or score, contact us using the address below and it will be removed.

Cookies

The game sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics scripts of any kind.

Data retention and your choices

Leaderboard rows persist until they fall out of the top 100 for their difficulty; daily-challenge rows stop being displayed after that day ends. To have a score removed, or to ask what is stored under a nickname, contact us at the address below. To remove everything held on your own device, clear site data for this site in your browser.

Where it runs

The game is hosted on a cloud platform, which processes the connection in order to deliver the site. Data may therefore be processed in the country that platform operates in. No data is sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
